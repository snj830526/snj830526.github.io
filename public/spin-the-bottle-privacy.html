Privacy Policy

Spin the Bottle: Bottle Game

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Spin the Bottle: Bottle Game does not require an account. The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or gameplay content.

Advertising and data collected by partners

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate coarse location;
  • device identifiers;
  • advertising data, such as ads shown;
  • product interaction data, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions; and
  • crash, performance, and other diagnostic data.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud and abuse, and perform analytics. Some information may be linked to a device. Processing is performed by Google and its partners under their own terms and privacy policies.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before ads are requested. A Privacy Options entry is available in the app when Google requires it for your region. You can also manage advertising and privacy permissions in your device settings.

The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the iOS advertising identifier unless Apple authorization is available. When consent or authorization is unavailable, non-personalized or limited ads may be served where applicable.
